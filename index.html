Privacy Policy

Hard Mode: 75 Day Challenge · Last updated June 29, 2026

Hard Mode is built to keep your data on your phone. There is no account, no cloud, and no server that stores your information. The short version: your challenge, your photos, and your record never leave your device, with one narrow exception for billing, explained below.

What we collect

Effectively nothing about you. Hard Mode has no login and no user accounts. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or location, and we have no server that could receive them.

The following are created and stored only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Your challenge terms, daily task checkmarks, and streak record (the ledger).
  • Your daily progress photos.
  • Your app settings and reminder preferences.

We cannot see any of this. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else.

Camera and photos

The app uses your camera so you can take a daily progress photo. These photos are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere. If you choose to share or save a proof card, that image is handed to your device's own share or photo-saving features, and what happens next is up to you.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device by your phone's operating system. No notification content is sent through any server.

Subscriptions and billing

This is the only part of the app that uses the internet. Subscriptions are handled by Apple's App Store and processed through RevenueCat, a service that manages subscription status on our behalf. When you start a subscription or restore a purchase, RevenueCat receives a randomly generated, anonymous app user ID along with purchase and receipt information and basic device details needed to verify your subscription. This is used solely to confirm whether your subscription is active. It is not used for advertising or tracking.

Your actual payment is processed by Apple under Apple's own terms. We never see or receive your card or payment details.

  • RevenueCat privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy

Analytics and advertising

None. Hard Mode contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. We do not build a profile of you and we do not sell data, because we do not collect it.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives only on your device, you are in full control of it. Deleting the app removes your challenge record, photos, and settings from your phone. There is nothing on a server for us to delete, because there is no server.

Children

Hard Mode is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from them. Since we collect no personal information at all, this app poses no data-collection risk to any user.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
